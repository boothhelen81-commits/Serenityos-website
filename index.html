What TrueMatch does

Finding a foundation that truly matches your skin is notoriously difficult. TrueMatch reads your skin tone directly from a photo and gives you clear guidance you can take into any store or use when shopping online.

  • 2. AI analysisTrueMatch analyses your skin tone in seconds.
